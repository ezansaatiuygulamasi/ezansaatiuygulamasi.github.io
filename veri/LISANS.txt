DISTRICT BOUNDARY DATA · Ezan Saati Uygulaması

Contains information from OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors), obtained via
OSM Boundaries (osm-boundaries.com) and geoBoundaries (geoboundaries.org, TUR ADM2,
TUR-ADM2-54988432), which is made available here under the Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0:
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/

The two files in this folder are the derived database the app uses to find which district
of Türkiye a location falls in. ilce-sinir.bin is identical to the file inside the app.
Changes: each shape was matched to a district in the app's catalogue; boundaries were
simplified with Douglas-Peucker while keeping shared edges between neighbours (tolerance
0.0002 degrees); coordinates were quantized to 0.00001 degrees (GeoJSON) and 0.00005
degrees (binary file).
